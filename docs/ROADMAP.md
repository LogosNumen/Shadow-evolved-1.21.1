# Roadmap: the story-accurate Dream Realm

Everything in Nightmare Evolved follows the web novel *Shadow Slave*: its ranks, Aspects, Flaws, Attributes, regions,
creatures and the road of Ascension. Facts are taken from the novel and its fan wiki (shadowslave.fandom.com,
CC-BY-SA); every description, tooltip and line of dialogue in the mod is original writing. No code or art comes from
other mods. Ideas from other Shadow Slave mods are welcome where the novel leaves gaps.

Each step below lands as its own pull request.

## 3a. Soul, Aspects, Flaws and Attributes (done)

- **The soul as the novel has it.** Every human has one soul core. Its rank rises with each Nightmare, and soul
  shards saturate it. Divine Aspects (Shadow Slave, Light Bringer, Mordret's mirrors) can grow up to seven cores,
  climbing the classes as creatures do, from Beast to Titan. Creatures drop one shard per core they had (their class),
  worth more the higher their rank.
- **Every Aspect in the wiki.** All named Aspects of the novel's Awakened, each at its own Aspect rank, from Dormant to
  Divine. Each has the abilities the novel gives it at each step: Dormant (Sleeper), Awakened, Ascended (Master), the
  Transformation (Saint), and the Supreme Ability and Domain (Sovereign). Where the novel leaves a step unknown, the
  mod fills it in the Aspect's spirit and says so.
- **Every Flaw in the wiki,** each paired with the Aspect it belongs to. Aspects whose Flaw the novel never names
  take one of the Flaws that suits them.
- **Every Attribute in the wiki.** The ones a soul is born with are rolled in the First Nightmare. Others are earned,
  like Dragonslayer, or come from lineage Memories, like The Weave, The Fire and The Blood. Some evolve as a soul
  grows: Mark of Divinity to Flame of Divinity, Child of Shadows to Lord of Shadows.
- **Runes:** a screen showing what the Spell knows of you: Name, Rank, Class, cores and fragments, Aspect and its
  abilities, Flaw, and Attributes.

## 3b. Soul Sea, Memories and Echoes (done; see [MEMORIES.md](MEMORIES.md))

- **The Soul Sea:** an inner sea under the star of each soul core, entered while the body stays aware.
  - Memories orbit there as lights, and Echoes stand there.
  - Shadow Slave keeps the shadows of the slain.
  - A Saint's claimed Citadel stands at its heart.
  - A Supreme can store items there.
- **Memories:** items of the soul, granted by the Spell for kills, rarer for rarer prey. Each has a rank (the
  creature's) and a tier (its class), and its own name and enchantments.
  - Kinds: weapons, layered armour, charms, tools, garments, consumable lineage Memories, and the Forgotten Shore's
    Shard Memories.
  - Summoning costs essence, and dismissing returns it. They mend themselves in the soul and are lost when their owner
    dies.
- **Echoes:** soulless copies of slain creatures, frozen as they died. They are summoned from the soul, fight with
  their Attributes, and heal over time.

## 3c. The Dream Realm (done; see [DREAM_REALM.md](DREAM_REALM.md))

- **A dimension of its own,** laid out region by region where the novel puts them: the Forgotten Shore and its Sea of
  Ash and barrows, the Burned Forest, the Hollow Mountains and the Underworld beneath them, the Nightmare Desert, the
  Moonriver Plain, Godgrave and the hollows inside its bones, the Glass Hell, the Chained Isles, Ravenheart, the Bone
  Sea, Bastion, the Weeping Goddess and the Stormsea.
- **Their landmarks:** the Crimson Spire and the Dark City, the Ashen Barrow, the Sanctuary of Noctis, the Night
  Temple, the Ivory and Ebony Towers, the Black Skull and the Tomb of Ariel, Nether's City, Vanishing Lake and the
  ziggurat of the Hollows, and the Citadels: Bastion, Ravenheart, Clan Sorrow's, Rivergate, Red Hill, the Night Garden
  and the Black Obelisk.
- **Their skies and laws:**
  - the Dark Sea that floods the Shore by night;
  - the Crushing of the Isles and their forbidden sky;
  - the mist of nothingness and the lightless dark;
  - the Desert's mirage and its one true path;
  - Godgrave's Cloudveil and the white sky behind it;
  - the Stormsea's looping courses, the Bone Sea's water, and the Glass Hell's burning lenses.
- **Getting there and back:**
  - A Sleeper's first sleep drops them somewhere random in the Dream Realm. Reaching a Gateway makes them Awakened and
    anchors them there.
  - Each side keeps its own belongings, and the sleeping body can be struck awake.
  - Masters cross in body, and Saints carry others across.
- **Seeds of Nightmare,** three of them the novel's own and the rest scattered by region, call to the Awakened. Each
  holds the Second through Sixth Nightmares by Category, and conquering one leaves a Gateway. The two knives cut the
  Dark Seed's chains. A Seed left alone blooms into a Gate in the waking world, with its waves and its Guardian.
- **Citadels:** a Saint binds one to their soul, and its likeness rises in their Soul Sea. The Great Citadels wait for
  Sovereigns.

## 3d. The creatures of the Dream Realm (done; see [CREATURES.md](CREATURES.md))

Sixty-six of the novel's Nightmare Creatures, region by region, at their ranks and classes, each with a body of its own
and moves to match. The greatest keep lairs at the places the novel puts them.

- **Forgotten Shore:** the Carapace Scavengers, Centurions and their Demon; the Black Knight; the Cursed Herald and the
  Crimson Terror on the Spire; Stone Saints, Iron Spiders and their Matriarch; the Lord of the Dead; Blood Fiends,
  Corpse Eaters and Flesh Reavers; the Soul Devouring Tree; and the Vile Thieving Bird's unhatched spawn.
- **Chained Isles:** Black Ravagers, Chain Worms, Chalice Maidens, Dread Wolves and Griffins; the Mirror Beast,
  Mordant Mimics, Sailor Dolls, Wormvine and the Blood-Nourished Root.
- **Hollow Mountains and the Underworld:** mist beings, the Saints of Tainted Jade, Absence, the Jade Titan and the
  Horror of the Lightless Current.
- **Nightmare Desert:** the Deathless, who rise each night and cannot die, and Skinwalkers.
- **Godgrave:** the Asuras of Condemnation and Condemnation itself; Abjuration; the Guardian of Vanishing Lake; the
  hollows' beasts, from blood mosquito swarms to the three-headed hound; ash worms; and the depth dweller of the
  Spinal Ocean, which now fills the spine's canal.
- **Burned Forest:** the Black Millipede tribe, its painted elders, and a Millipede Queen who slips between moments,
  in a fortified hive.
- **Elsewhere:** Chalice Knights and stone worms around Ravenheart, the gaunt maw of the Moonriver Plain, the
  Undetectable Forest Maw around Bastion, and the Stormsea's serpents and Old Tom.
- **Gates** send out the creatures of their Seed's own region.

Still to come: the Corrupted Immortals of the Eternal City, the Cloudveil's fireflies, and creatures for the Glass Hell,
the Bone Sea, the Weeping Goddess and the Land of Barrows.

## 3e. Ascension (under way; see [ASCENSION.md](ASCENSION.md))

- **Saint Transformations** drawn on the code-built creature rig, each matching the novel: Kai's midnight dragon,
  Tyris's thunderbird, Roan's winged lion, Helie's centaur, Silent Stalker's panther, Noctis's wolf of moonlight, Jest's
  horned fiend, Seishan's shark-skinned kin, Revel's bat-winged form and Ki Song's tide of blood; Morgan's living steel,
  Jet's mist, the Summer Knight's sunlight, Nephis's radiance and Sunny's shadow. (Done.)
- **The rank gap:** lower souls barely harm higher ones and, far enough below, can't move them. (Done.)
- **Fealty and Sovereign Domains:** Saints swear to Sovereigns, and Domains spread through the Citadels a Sovereign or
  their sworn Saints have bound; a Saint without a liege has a nascent Domain. The innate Domains (swords for Anvil, the
  dead for Ki Song, a branded word for Kai) came with the Aspects in 3a. (Done.)
- **Citadel Components:** the powers a Citadel's lord commands, once the novel's details are confirmed.
- Every Saint flying and every Sovereign leading a legion, from the earlier overhaul, don't match the novel: flight
  comes with the winged forms, and armies move to the Great Clans below.
