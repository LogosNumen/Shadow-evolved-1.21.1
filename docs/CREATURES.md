# The creatures of the Dream Realm

Every region of the Dream Realm has its own Nightmare Creatures, each at the rank and class the novel gives it. Where
the novel names a creature but gives no rank, or gives a rank but little of its look, the mod chooses, and says so
below. All descriptions in the mod are original writing.

## How they live

- **Roaming creatures** come out around Sleepers and the Awakened: up to twelve within reach of anyone in the realm.
  Each kind keeps to its region, or to a part of it (Godgrave's hollows, the Underworld), and some keep to the day or
  the night. Pack creatures come in groups. Fliers come out of the air, and swimmers come out of the water.
- **Lairs:** the greatest creatures each keep one place and are there when someone comes within about a hundred
  blocks. A keeper slain leaves its lair empty for seven days. Keepers bear their rank and class in their name.
- **Rank and class:** a creature's rank (Dormant to Unholy) and class (Beast to Titan) set its strength and what it
  leaves behind: one soul shard for each core it had. A few have no soul at all and leave nothing.
- **Their bodies** are built in code, each from its own bones and painted by hand, with moves that wind up before they
  land: bites, sweeps, charges, grabs, shots and calls to their kin.
- **Gates:** a Seed of Nightmare that blooms sends the creatures of its own region through its Gate, and the strongest
  of them comes last as the Gate's Guardian.
