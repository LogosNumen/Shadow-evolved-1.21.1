# Aspects, Flaws and Attributes

Every Aspect, Flaw and Attribute in Nightmare Evolved, generated from the mod itself. Names come from the novel; where
the novel never names an Aspect, it goes by its bearer's title or what it does. Abilities marked *filled* are ones the
novel never shows: the mod filled the gap in the Aspect's spirit. Flaws marked *fitting* belong to Aspects whose Flaw
the novel never names; one of them is rolled. All descriptions are the mod's own words.

## Flaws

| Flaw | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Clear Conscience | You cannot lie, and a question put to you must be answered. Leave one hanging and the strain grows into pain until you speak. |
| Pristine Soul | You must suffer to use your power: every use burns you as if you were alive in a fire. The pain never kills. |
| Crown of Thorns | You cannot kill. Your blows stop a breath short of death, and what you fell only collapses. |
| Blindness | You are blind. As your Aspect grows, it becomes your sight. |
| Ear for Lies | You know every lie, and the ugliness behind it. Deceivers near you (the unseen, mimics, illusions) sicken you, though you see them plainly. |
| Ravenous Body | Your body needs far more than others'. You hunger quickly, and weaken when you go without. |
| Deathly Core | Your soul is dead and your core broken. It makes no essence, leaks what it holds, and shards do nothing for it; only killing feeds it. Empty, you wither like a corpse. |
| Sevenfold Self | Your self is broken into seven pieces. Now and then another piece holds one of your powers, and it will not answer you. |
| Brittle Touch | Your touch makes things fragile. What you wield wears out far faster. |
| Forsaken Treasures | You must lose what you cherish. Things you have given names wear away while you carry them. |
| A Mother's Love | You love those who serve you. When one of them falls, the grief costs you strength and essence. |
| Borrowed Time | Each use of your power shortens your life: it takes a little of your health, which returns only slowly. |
| Endless Growth | Your body never stops growing. You grow larger and heavier with time, and slower for it. |
| Bare Hands | You cannot use anything enchanted. Whatever enchanted thing you hold or wear slips from you. |
| Binding Word | You cannot break a promise. Crouch and touch a creature with an empty hand to swear peace to it; you will never be able to harm it. |
| Rash Heart | You are poor at holding back. Struck, you throw yourself at whatever struck you. |
| Still Waters | Your Aspect answers only while your emotions are in check. Freshly hurt, or close to death, it falls silent. |
| Overlooked | You are naturally unassuming. No one sees your name above you. |
| Forgotten Name | Everyone forgets your name, however often you tell them. |
| Unspoken | The novel says only that this Flaw is plain to see. Here, your essence returns slowly when no one is near you. |
| Slow Well | Your soul refills its essence slowly. |
| Hot Blood | Every blow you take stokes your temper, until you charge in, stronger but heedless of your guard. |
| Lightless | You cannot step into sunlight. Under the open daytime sky you burn. |
| Bloodthirst | You crave blood. The taste of it drives you on; go too long without, and you weaken. |
| Landbound | Your power belongs to the water. On dry land it costs twice as much and you move heavily. |
