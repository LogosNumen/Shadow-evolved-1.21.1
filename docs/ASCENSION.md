# Ascension

What changes as a soul climbs past the Master rank: Saints' Transformations, the gap between ranks, and Sovereigns'
Domains. Everything here follows *Shadow Slave*; all descriptions in the mod are original writing.

## Transformations

At Saint (Transcendent), every Aspect's fourth ability wakes: its Transformation, on the **V** key. A transformed
Saint is stronger, larger, often swifter, and burns essence for as long as the form is held; pressing the key again
lets it go. Everyone nearby sees the form.

Ten Saints become a creature of their own, drawn in place of their body:

| Aspect | Holder | Form |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Nightingale | Kai | A dragon with midnight scales and swept horns, on great wings |
| Sky Tide | Tyris | A white thunderbird with steel talons, lightning along its wings |
| Golden Thunder | Roan | A white lion with amber eyes, on white wings |
| Huntress's Bow | Helie | A centaur, her bow in hand |
| Silent Stalker | Silent Stalker | A huge black panther |
| Moonlight | Noctis | A pale wolf of moonlight with a crescent on its brow |
| Kindled Passions | Jest | A horned, goat-legged fiend |
| Crimson Hunger | Seishan | A tall, shark-skinned monster |
| Lightslayer | Revel | A dark, lean body on bat's wings |
| Queen of Worms | Ki Song | A turning tide of blood, her shape at its crest |

Five keep their own body, changed:

| Aspect | Holder | Form |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Princess of War | Morgan | Living steel |
| Soul Reaper | Jet | A wraith of mist |
| Summer Knight | Gilead | A body of sunlight |
| Light Bringer | Nephis | Radiant, with wings of light |
| Shadow Slave | Sunny | A shadow |

The rest change nothing the eye can see beyond what their Transformation does: Effie's giantess simply grows, and
Cassie's sight, Mordret's reflections and the Beastmaster's ruinous beauty work unseen. Winged forms fly, folding their
wings on the ground and beating them aloft. In a creature's body, the first-person hand is gone.

## The rank gap

Players and Nightmare Creatures stand on one ladder: a Sleeper with Dormant creatures, an Awakened with Awakened ones,
a Master with the Fallen, a Saint with the Corrupted, a Sovereign with the Great, and so on. A higher soul is of a
different order from those below it:

- A player struck from one rank below takes 60% of the blow, from two ranks 30%, and from three or more 12%.
- A creature struck by a lower player takes a quarter less per rank of the gap, so the bold can still fight upward.
- A higher soul's blows land a quarter harder per rank.
- From three ranks below, an attacker can no longer knock its target back.

## Fealty and Domains

A Sovereign's **Domain** is the Dream Realm they rule, carried by Citadels. Every Citadel holds the land around its
Gateway for someone:

| Citadel | Whose Domain | How far |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A Great Citadel (Bastion, Ravenheart, the Night Garden) | The Sovereign bound to it | About 192 blocks |
| A lesser Citadel bound by a Sovereign | That Sovereign | About 96 blocks |
| A lesser Citadel bound by a Saint sworn to a Sovereign | The Saint's liege | About 96 blocks |
| A lesser Citadel bound by a Saint who serves no one | The Saint's own **nascent Domain** | About 32 blocks |

Within their own Domain a Sovereign takes only a fifth of each blow and cannot be knocked back, and a ring of light
follows their steps. A Saint in their nascent Domain takes seven tenths. Anyone who crosses into or out of a Domain is
told whose it is. Where Domains overlap, a Sovereign's outweighs a nascent one, and otherwise the nearer Citadel holds.

**Fealty:** a Saint offers their fealty with `/spell swear <sovereign>`, and the Sovereign takes it with
`/spell receive <saint>` (or by clicking the offer). `/spell renounce` breaks a Saint's own oath, and a Sovereign
releases a Saint with `/spell renounce <saint>`. `/spell status` names a Saint's liege. Oaths last while either player
is away.

Some Aspects have a Domain of their own besides, as Supreme abilities: the King of Swords rules everyone who wields a
sword, the Queen of Worms the dead she gathers, and Kai can brand a word into a soul.

## Not yet

- **Citadel Components**, the powers a Citadel's lord commands. The novel names few of them (Ravenheart's reshapes the
  land, and the Night Garden's anchors both ends of a Dream Gate); they wait until their details are confirmed.
- **Corruption:** in the novel, Domains built on Citadels shield their people from Corruption. This mod has no
  Corruption for players yet.
- **Legions and flight:** the novel gives neither every Sovereign a legion nor every Saint flight. Flight comes from
  the winged forms above, and armies belong with the Great Clans in phase 4.
