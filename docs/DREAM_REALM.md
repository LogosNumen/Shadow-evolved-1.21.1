# The Dream Realm

The Dream Realm is a dimension of its own. Its map is the same in every world: each region, landmark, Gateway and canon
Seed stands where the novel puts it. The terrain's finer detail and the scattered Seeds come from the world seed.
Everything here follows *Shadow Slave*; all descriptions in the mod are original writing.

## The map

The Forgotten Shore lies in the north. From there the realm runs south, with the Chained Isles in the east and the
Stormsea past everything in the far south.

## Landmarks

- **Forgotten Shore:** the Crimson Spire with its Gateway on the high balcony; the Dark City, with Bright Castle, the
  Catacombs, the Ruined Cathedral and the Library ruins; the Ashen Barrow and its tree; Bone Ridge; and the
  Underground Mine with its black river.
- **Chained Isles:** the Sanctuary of Noctis, with its menhirs, pool, ancient tree and white altar; the Northern
  Island, and the Night Temple hanging under it with its black altar; the Ivory and Ebony Towers; Iron Hand, Reckoning
  and the Shipwreck, whose hold keeps a chest; the Desecrated Grove; and the Twisted Trees, Jagged Rocks and the
  Corrupted Island.
- **Hollow Mountains:** the Black Mountain, the temple gate of the Underworld, and Nether's City.
- **Nightmare Desert:** the Black Skull, the Tomb of Ariel, its gauntlet and the mirage path.
- **Godgrave:** the skull, Vanishing Lake's hidden pond, the ziggurat of the Hollows and the ruined cities around it.
- **Citadels:** Bastion, Ravenheart, Clan Sorrow's citadel, Rivergate, Red Hill, the Night Garden and the Black Obelisk.

## Coming and going

- **The first sleep.** A Sleeper's first sleep after the First Nightmare drops them somewhere at random: always on safe
  ground, never in a Death Zone, and above the night's sea on the Shore. Nothing from the waking world comes with them.
- **Gateways.** A soul reaches a Gateway by standing in the Crimson Spire's ring, touching an altar or a Gateway's
  heart, stepping into Vanishing Lake's pond, or coming near the Night Temple's black altar. Its body glows brighter
  and brighter, then fades away. A Sleeper who passes through comes back Awakened. Whatever Gateway a soul last
  passed through is its anchor, and every later sleep brings it there.
- **Two sets of belongings.** What a dreamer finds in the Dream Realm waits there for the next dream, and what they own
  in the waking world stays with their sleeping body. The `separateDreamBelongings` game rule turns this off.
- **The sleeping body** lies in its bed and can be found. If it is struck, the dreamer wakes at once and the blow
  lands.
- **Crossing in body.** A Master can cross between the worlds in body by holding still for five seconds (**L**),
  taking everything with them and leaving a tether to return to. A Saint is a living Gateway: anyone crouching close
  beside them is carried along.

`/ne realm dream`, `wake` and `cross` do the same at once, `/ne realm state` shows what a soul's journeys have left in
it, and `/ne realm tp <x> <z>` goes anywhere.

## Seeds of Nightmare

From the Second Nightmare on, a soul's next Nightmare waits inside a Seed of Nightmare of its Category.

- **Where they grow.** Three are the novel's own: the Dark Seed, chained with seven shackles at the top of the Ivory
  Tower (Category Two); the block of black stone at the heart of the Tomb of Ariel (Category Three); and the Seed on
  the ziggurat in Godgrave's hollow (Category Two). The rest are scattered a few to a region, with many in the
  Nightmare Desert. Each stands on a dais of black stone, at a Category that suits its region.
- **The Call.** An Awakened near an unconquered Seed hears it now and then, with its Category and direction.
  Stronger souls hear it from farther away.
- **Challenge.** Touching a Seed takes a soul into its Nightmare if it is the soul's next one. A Seed has nothing to
  say to a soul that is not yet Awakened, that is already past its Nightmare, or that is not yet ready for it.
- **Conquest.** Winning destroys the Seed and leaves a Gateway in its place. The Dark Seed's becomes the Ivory Tower's
  Citadel.
- **The Dark Seed.** Inside it, the Guardian cannot be harmed except by a soul carrying one of the two knives that cut
  its chains, and a soul carrying both strikes harder. The Night Temple's black altar gives the ivory knife for blood
  shed on it. The Sanctuary's white altar lets the obsidian knife go for a thousand of the Isles' strange gold coins,
  laid on it under the night sky. Nightmare Creatures of the Isles drop the coins, and the Shipwreck's chest holds
  some.

## Gates

A Seed someone has found, if left alone for six days, blooms (the `nightmareGates` game rule turns this off):

1. Every Awakened is warned that the air of the waking world has begun to bend wrongly.
2. Somewhere near a player, a tall, faint warp gathers for a minute.
3. It tears open into a ragged rift of darkness with a burning edge. Touching it is deadly.
4. Waves of Nightmare Creatures pour through, stronger as the Gate grows, until the **Guardian of the Gate** comes,
   a rank above the rest.
5. Slaying the Guardian closes the Gate, and the Seed sleeps until it ripens again. Conquering the Seed's Nightmare
   closes it for good.

An Awakened who sleeps near an open Gate is drawn by its Call to the Seed it grew from.

`/ne seed near [range]` lists the Seeds around you, and `/ne seed go <id>` goes to one. `/ne seed bloom <id>` opens its
Gate near you, and `/ne seed conquer <id>` counts it as conquered.

## Citadels

A Citadel is a human stronghold built around a conquered Gateway. A Saint binds one to their soul by crouching and
touching its Gateway. From then on:

- it answers to them alone, and no one else can bind it while they hold it;
- its likeness stands at the heart of their Soul Sea, built of the Citadel's own stone;
- its Gateway is their anchor.

The Great Citadels (Bastion, Ravenheart and the Night Garden) are meant for Sovereigns and refuse a Saint. A soul
holds one Citadel at a time, and binding another frees the first. The Citadels' Components and the Sovereigns'
Domains come with Ascension (3e).

`/ne realm bind` binds the Citadel of the Gateway beside you, and `/ne realm release` lets it go.
