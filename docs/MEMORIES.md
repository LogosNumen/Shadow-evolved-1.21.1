# Memories and Echoes

Every Memory the mod knows, as the Spell would show its runes. Facts follow the novel and its fan wiki; every description
here and in the game is original writing. Where the novel shows a Memory but not its powers, the mod gives it some in its
spirit and marks it *filled*.

## How Memories work

- **Where they come from.** The Spell gives a Memory for a kill now and then: more often for rarer prey (a creature's
  class), always for a trial's guardian, and less often the higher the prey's Rank. A creature the novel names leaves its
  own Memory the first time. The First Nightmare ends with a Memory woven from its guardian, and often one of the small
  Memories the novel's Sleepers carry out of theirs.
- **Rank and Tier.** A Memory's Rank is its prey's (a Dormant creature leaves a Dormant Memory, a Fallen one an
  Ascended Memory, and so on up), and its Tier is its prey's class: an Awakened Tyrant leaves a Tier V Awakened Memory.
  Higher Tiers hold more enchantments: one at Tier I, two from III, three from V, four at VII.
- **Summoning and dismissing.** Summoning costs essence, more for higher Ranks and Tiers and much more above your own
  Rank; dismissing a Memory nearby gives the essence back. Weapons and tools appear in the hand, armour goes straight on
  (one layer at a time, if you like), and charms, garments, crowns and masks are simply worn. A Memory left too far away
  when dismissed wastes its essence. Resting in the soul, a Memory slowly mends.
- **Bound to a soul.** A Memory answers only its owner. It changes hands only by touch: crouch and use it on another
  Awakened to give it. A copy left over from an earlier summoning dissolves.
- **Death.** As in the novel, Memories and Echoes die with their owner, all but the seven Shard Memories, which are
  left behind for anyone to take up. The game rule `keepSoulMemories` keeps them instead. Dying inside a Nightmare is a
  failed trial, not a death, and loses nothing.
- **The Oath Keys.** Once all seven Shard Memories rest in one soul, the Oath Keys take shape from them.

Press **J** to tend your Memories, Echoes and Soul Sea, **G** to dismiss the Memory in your hand.

## The novel's Memories

### Dormant

- **Endless Spring**: Dormant, Tier IV tool. A bottle of patterned blue glass that is never quite empty for long.
  - [Gift of Water] Drink from it, or pour out water. It refills over time.
  - [Blessing of Flesh] The body tires more slowly and recovers sooner.
- **Silver Bell**: Dormant, Tier II tool. A small silver bell. Rung, it carries much farther than it has any right to.
  - [Silver Song] Rung, it is heard across a vast distance.
  - [Sonorous] Its owner chooses how loud it rings (crouch to ring softly).
- **Dream Blade**: Dormant, Tier I weapon. A plain silver longsword. It guards the soul a little, which is more than most swords do.
  - [Dreamer's Ward] Attacks on the soul land softer while it is held.
- **Evertwine**: Dormant, Tier I tool (*filled*). A coil of golden rope that always seems to reach exactly as far as it needs to.
  - [Golden Twine] It pulls its owner to what they point at, or binds a creature fast.

### Awakened

- **Midnight Shard**: Awakened, Tier III weapon, from the Carapace Demon (Shard Memory). A tachi of bright star-metal on a hilt of black wood. It does not break, and it does not yield.
  - [Unbroken] Near impossible to break; fiercest when its wielder is nearly dead.
- **Zenith Shard**: Awakened, Tier V weapon, from the Iron Spider Matriarch (Shard Memory, *filled*). A bronze spear, its head forged from a fallen star, lighter than it looks.
  - [Zenith] It strikes hardest falling on its foe from above.
- **Azure Blade**: Awakened, Tier I weapon, from the Carapace Scavenger. A straight blade the colour of a clear sky, cut from a scavenger's shell.
- **Starlight Legion Armour**: Awakened, Tier II armour, from the Carapace Centurion. The bright armour of a legion that once fought on the Forgotten Shore.
- **Dark Wing**: Awakened, Tier I garment, from the Flesh Reaver. A cloak as clear and light as a dragonfly's wings, and as hard to tear.
  - [Glide] Drift down softly, hang above the ground (crouch in the air), skim water.
- **Puppeteer's Shroud**: Awakened, Tier V armour, from the Mountain King. Dark grey silk and soft boots, woven from what a worm of doubt left behind.
  - [Enhanced Durability] It lasts far longer.
  - [Doubtless] A little protection against what would get into the mind.
  - [Blessing of Spirit] The mind tires more slowly and recovers sooner.
- **Blood Blossom**: Awakened, Tier II charm, from the Blood Flower. A red flower that never wilts. Everything its owner commands grows thirsty.
  - [Flower of Evil] Its owner's Memories, Echoes and Shadows thirst for blood, and grow stronger.
- **Broken Oath**: Awakened, Tier I charm, from the Golem Lord. A cracked charm. Standing near whoever wears it wears the soul down.
  - [Oathbreaker] It wears away the soul of anyone who comes near its wearer.
- **Prowling Thorn**: Awakened, Tier II weapon. A thorny dagger on a string no one can see. It always comes back. Almost always.
  - [Rose of Betrayal] Throw it; an unseen string brings it back. Now and then it snaps.
- **Extraordinary Rock**: Awakened, Tier I tool, from the Rolling Stone. It is a rock. It is, however, an extraordinary one.
  - [Not Really] Throw it. It lands far harder than any rock should.
- **Heavenly Burden**: Awakened, Tier III tool. A golden needle. Whatever it pierces forgets how to stay on the ground.
  - [Unburdened] What it pierces rises into the sky. Crouch and use it to rise yourself.
- **Essence Quiver**: Awakened, Tier II tool (*filled*). A quiver that is never empty, as long as its owner has essence to spare.
  - [Essence Arrows] Arrows woven from essence, one essence apiece.
- **Silent Dagger**: Awakened, Tier I weapon. A slim dark dagger. Nothing its bearer does makes a sound.
  - [Hush] Its bearer makes no sound; its first cut finds the unwary.
- **Enchanted Bag**: Awakened, Tier I tool. A leather bag far bigger inside than out. It does tend to spill.
  - [Bigger Inside] Holds far more than it should, but spills it all when dismissed.

## Memories the Spell weaves

For prey the novel names no Memory for, the Spell weaves one in a shape that suits the creature, names it for the kind
of creature and the shape ("Hollow Fang", "Chitin Veil"), and rolls its enchantments for its Tier.

- **Woven Blade** (weapon): A blade the Spell wove from a slain creature.
- **Woven Spear** (weapon): A spear the Spell wove from a slain creature.
- **Woven Axe** (weapon): An axe the Spell wove from a slain creature.
- **Woven Dagger** (weapon): A dagger the Spell wove from a slain creature.
- **Woven Bow** (weapon): A bow the Spell wove from a slain creature.
- **Woven Shield** (weapon): A shield the Spell wove from a slain creature.
- **Bone Mail** (armour): Armour of bone, woven from a slain creature.
- **Chitin Mail** (armour): Armour of chitin, woven from a slain creature.
- **Plate Mail** (armour): Armour of dark plate, woven from a slain creature.
- **Woven Charm** (charm): A charm the Spell wove from a slain creature.
- **Woven Cloak** (garment): A cloak the Spell wove from a slain creature.
- **Woven Lantern** (tool): A lantern the Spell wove from a slain creature.
- **Woven Canteen** (tool): A canteen the Spell wove from a slain creature.
- **Woven Horn** (tool): A horn the Spell wove from a slain creature.

- Weapons may roll: [Keen], [Hungering], [Venomous], [Searing], [Frostbitten], [Rending], [Thundering], [Weighty], [Ghost Edge].
- Armour may roll: [Sturdy], [Warding], [Steadfast], [Flameproof], [Lightfoot].
- Charms may roll: [Vigor], [Wellspring], [Clear Mind], [Night Eyes], [Fleet], [Fortune].
- Garments may roll: [Shrouded], [Weatherward], [Softstep].
- Any Memory may roll: [Durable], [Swift Summoning], [Tethered], [Mending Weave].

## Echoes and Shadows

- An Echo is a frozen copy of a slain creature: made of essence, physical only when summoned, with a little will of its
  own and no soul. It fights for its owner, never changes, heals while it rests, and if it is killed, it is gone for good.
  The Spell gives them rarely, for kills: more often for rarer prey, and a guardian one time in four.
- How many can be out at once depends on Rank: one for a Sleeper, up to twelve for the Divine. Summoning one costs essence,
  given back when it is dismissed nearby.
- The Spell reads Attributes in each Echo:
  - [Strong] Hits much harder.
  - [Armored] Much harder to hurt.
  - [Swift] Moves quickly.
  - [Battle Master] Skilled in every kind of fighting.
  - [Stalwart] Shrugs off harm, and nothing clouds its mind.
  - [Relentless] Recovers whenever it is not fighting.
  - [Venomous] Its attacks poison.
  - [Keen Senses] Notices foes from far off.
  - [Shadow Sworn] Known to shadows; stronger in the dark.
  - [Dark Destrier] A steed for a shadow warrior, swifter and stronger in the dark.
  - [Soul Weapon] A weapon at heart; its blows land harder.
  - [Soul Beast] A beast of the soul that grows with its master.
- **Shadows.** Sunny's Aspect keeps the shadow of everything he slays standing in his Soul Sea. If an Echo's own kind
  stands there, he can spend a hundred shadow fragments to make it a Shadow: it grows with his Shadow Core, and a badly
  hurt one falls into a long sleep instead of dying. He can hold one Shadow for each of his cores.

## The Soul Sea

- Press **K** to meditate (stand still, out of a fight) and sink into your own Soul Sea; press it again to rise. Your body
  stays sitting where you left it, still aware of what goes on around it: whatever hunts nearby may find it, and the
  moment it is struck, you are torn back into it.
- Your cores hang over the water as suns, one for each, brighter as they fill. Your Memories circle the heart of the sea
  as little lights (touch one to summon or dismiss it), and your Echoes stand in a ring around them.
- Each sea is its owner's: Sunny's is dark and silent under black suns, with the shadows of the slain standing guard;
  Nephis's is lit gold under blazing white stars; Mordret's lies under grey cloud in a white mist, its pale suns marked;
  an island rises out of Effie's.
- A Saint's soul surpasses its vessel: the Citadel bound to it stands at the heart of its sea, as a likeness.
- A Sovereign's sea can keep plain things as well as Memories (six rows of them).
